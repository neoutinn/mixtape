Mixtape

Put your Spotify playlists on your iPod. Paste a link, press a button, done.

Download Mixtape

Free. Works with iPods running Rockbox.

How it works

1

Open Mixtape

Double-click the file you downloaded. A page opens in your web browser.

2

Plug in your iPod

Mixtape finds it by itself and shows how much space is free.

3

Paste a playlist

Copy the playlist's link in Spotify, paste it in, and press Put it on my iPod.

Then on the iPod, open Playlists from the main menu. Your playlist is there with album covers.

Opening it the first time

Your computer will warn you because Mixtape is a small free app, not one from a big company. This only happens once.

  1. Double-click Mixtape-Windows.exe (it's in your Downloads folder).
  2. If a blue box says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. A page opens in your browser. That's Mixtape!
  1. Open your Downloads folder and double-click the .zip file. A Mixtape app appears.
  2. Drag Mixtape into your Applications folder, then double-click it.
  3. If it says “Apple could not verify…”, click Done. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to Mixtape.
  4. A page opens in your browser. That's Mixtape!

Not sure which Mac download? Click the Apple menu → About This Mac. If it says Chip: Apple M…, get “Apple Silicon”. If it says Processor: Intel, get “Intel”.

  1. Make it runnable: chmod +x Mixtape-Linux (or right-click → Properties → “Allow executing”).
  2. Double-click it, or run ./Mixtape-Linux.

Questions

Where does the music come from?

Spotify doesn't let anyone download its songs, so Mixtape reads the list of songs from your playlist and finds each one on YouTube, picking the version whose length matches the Spotify one. It then adds the song name, artist, album and cover. Once in a while it picks a slightly different version (like a live one). If that happens, let whoever set this up know.

Some songs say “couldn't be added”

A few songs, usually very new or very obscure ones, can't be found. Press Update on the playlist in a few days to try again.

I added songs to the playlist in Spotify. How do I get them on the iPod?

Open Mixtape, plug in the iPod, and press Update next to the playlist. Only the new songs get downloaded.

My playlist has more than 100 songs

Spotify only shares the first 100 songs of a link. For bigger playlists, go to exportify.app, log in, click Export next to the playlist, and drop the downloaded file into Mixtape (under “My playlist has more than 100 songs”).

Will it delete my other music?

No. Mixtape only adds files. When you remove a playlist in Mixtape, it only deletes songs that Mixtape added for that playlist and that aren't in another playlist.

The songs don't show up under Artists or Albums on the iPod

Playlists work right away. To also browse by artist or album, on the iPod go to Settings → General Settings → Database → Update Now once after adding music. You can turn on Auto Update there too.

Mixtape doesn't see my iPod

Check that the iPod screen shows it's connected by USB, not just charging. Try another cable or USB port. In Mixtape, click It's plugged in but nothing shows up for more help.

How do I close Mixtape?

Click Close Mixtape at the bottom of the page, or just close the browser tab. It shuts itself down a few minutes later.
