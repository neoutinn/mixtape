      Add a Spotify playlist

      Where do I get the link?
      1. Open the playlist in Spotify (phone or computer).
      2. Tap the ••• button (or right-click the playlist).
      3. Choose Share → Copy link.
      4. Come back here, click the box above and paste (Ctrl+V, or ⌘+V on a Mac).

      Albums and single songs work too.

      Spotify only lets us see the first 100 songs of a playlist. If yours is longer, see “My playlist has more than 100 songs” below.

      Plug in your iPod first (step 1).
